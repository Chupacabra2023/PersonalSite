Privacy Policy for Phone4All

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Phone4All application handles permissions and processes data during use of the app.

2. Data Collection

Phone4All is designed with user privacy as a priority. The application:

  • Does not collect any personal data
  • Does not require user registration or account creation
  • Does not transmit any data over the internet
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services
  • Does not contain advertising

All 12 mini-games included in the app are played locally on a single device, with players and teams taking turns passing the phone around. No player names, scores, or other information entered during gameplay ever leave the device.

3. Permissions Used

Phone4All is a fully offline party game and does not require special or sensitive device permissions (such as location, contacts, camera, or microphone access) to function. Any standard permissions the app may request (for example, vibration or sound for game feedback) are used solely to provide the in-game experience and are never used to collect or transmit personal data.

4. Local Data Storage

Any game settings (such as team names, selected mini-games, or sound/vibration preferences) are stored locally on your device only.

  • Settings and in-game data are stored only on your device
  • No data is backed up to any server
  • Uninstalling the app removes all stored data

5. What the Application Does NOT Do

To protect user privacy, the application explicitly states that it:

  • Does not collect personal data of any kind
  • Does not access your contacts, messages, photos, or other personal files
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not record audio or access your camera
  • Does not share any data with third parties
  • Does not contain advertising
  • Does not require an internet connection
  • Does not include hidden background tracking or recording behavior

6. User Control and Rights

Users have full control over their data:

  • Any locally saved settings can be cleared by clearing the app's data in device settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data

7. Children's Privacy

Phone4All does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. As a party game intended for group play, parental guidance is recommended based on the app's content rating.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If changes are made to how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
