Privacy Policy for Snowball Roll Down

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Snowball Roll Down game application handles permissions and processes data during use of the app.

2. Data Collection

Snowball Roll Down is designed with user privacy in mind. The application:

  • Does not require user registration or account creation
  • Does not collect personal identification information
  • Stores game progress and high scores locally on your device

3. Game Data Storage

All game-related data, including:

  • High scores - stored locally on your device
  • Game progress and upgrades - saved locally
  • User preferences and settings - kept on device only

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers by the application itself.

4. Leaderboard Features

If the application includes online leaderboard functionality through Google Play Games Services, your gameplay data (such as scores) may be shared with Google's services to enable global rankings. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. What the Application Does NOT Do

To protect user privacy, the application explicitly states that it:

  • Does not collect personal data beyond what is necessary for gameplay
  • Does not access your contacts, messages, or other personal files
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not record audio or access your camera
  • Does not share personal data with third parties (except for advertising services as described below)

6. Advertising

The application displays advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob.

These services may use device identifiers, including the Advertising ID, to display relevant or personalized advertisements.

The application itself does not collect or store personal data for advertising purposes. Any data used for advertising is handled according to the privacy policies of the respective advertising providers. Users can opt out of personalized ads in their device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Snowball Roll Down is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not require any personal data to play.

8. User Control and Rights

Users have full control over their data:

  • Game data can be cleared through device settings or by uninstalling the app
  • Advertising preferences can be managed in device settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If changes are made to how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
