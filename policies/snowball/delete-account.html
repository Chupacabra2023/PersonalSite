Delete Account & Data - Snowball Roll Down

Important: Snowball Roll Down does not create user accounts on external servers. All your game data is stored locally on your device.

How Your Data is Stored

Snowball Roll Down stores all game data locally on your device, including:

  • High scores and game progress
  • Purchased upgrades and unlocked items
  • Game settings and preferences

We do not have access to this data as it never leaves your device.

How to Delete All Local Game Data

To completely remove all Snowball Roll Down data from your device, simply uninstall the app:

1 Android: Go to Settings > Apps > Snowball Roll Down > Uninstall
2 Alternative: Long-press the app icon and select "Uninstall"
Note: Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all your game progress, high scores, and settings. This action cannot be undone.

Google Play Games Services Data

If you have connected the game to Google Play Games Services for leaderboards or achievements, your data may also be stored by Google. To manage or delete this data:

Manage Google Account Data

In your Google Account settings, you can:

  • View and delete Play Games data
  • Remove game activity from your account
  • Manage connected apps and services
