Privacy Policy for Time Reminder

Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Time Reminder application handles permissions and processes data during use of the app.

2. Data Collection

Time Reminder is designed with user privacy as a priority. The application:

  • Does not collect any personal data
  • Does not require user registration or account creation
  • Does not transmit any data over the internet
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services

3. Permissions Used

The application requires certain device permissions to function properly:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to display alerts when a timer finishes. The notification appears in the device's notification bar to inform you that your set time has elapsed.
  • Display over other apps / Full-screen intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Required to show a full-screen alert when a timer expires, even when the device is locked. This ensures you are notified immediately when your timer completes.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Required to turn on the screen when a timer finishes, so you can see the alert even if the device screen was off.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to ensure timers trigger at the exact time they are set to expire.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Required to keep timers running accurately in the background while you use other apps.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used to vibrate the device when a timer finishes.

Important: All permissions are used solely for timer functionality. No permission is used to collect, store, or transmit personal data.

4. Local Data Storage

All timer data, including timer names and duration settings, is stored locally on the device. No data is transmitted to external servers or cloud services.

  • Timer configurations are stored only on your device
  • No data is backed up to any server
  • Uninstalling the app removes all stored data

5. What the Application Does NOT Do

To protect user privacy, the application explicitly states that it:

  • Does not collect personal data of any kind
  • Does not access your contacts, messages, photos, or other personal files
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not record audio or access your camera
  • Does not share any data with third parties
  • Does not contain advertising
  • Does not require an internet connection
  • Does not include hidden background tracking or recording behavior

6. User Control and Rights

Users have full control over their data and permissions:

  • All permissions can be revoked at any time via device settings
  • Timer data can be cleared by clearing the app's data in device settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data

7. Children's Privacy

Time Reminder is suitable for users of all ages. The application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If changes are made to how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
