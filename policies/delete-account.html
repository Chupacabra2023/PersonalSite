Data Deletion Request – Soundboard Studio

Soundboard Studio does not collect any personal data directly. The only data collected is the Advertising ID, which is managed by Google AdMob.

To delete or reset your Advertising ID:

  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Go to Privacy → Ads
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID
