Delete Data — Soundboard Studio

No account required. Soundboard Studio does not create user accounts and does not store any data on external servers. All your data (recordings, button configurations, preferences) lives exclusively on your device.

What Data the App Stores

Soundboard Studio stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Audio recordings created with the built-in recorder
  • Imported audio files and their metadata
  • Button names, colors, and category assignments
  • App preferences and settings

None of this data is ever transmitted to or stored on external servers.

How to Delete All App Data

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it entirely by uninstalling the app:

1 Option A — Uninstall the app: Go to Settings > Apps > Soundboard Studio > Uninstall. This permanently removes the app and all its data.
2 Option B — Clear app data only (keep the app): Go to Settings > Apps > Soundboard Studio > Storage > Clear Data. This deletes all recordings, buttons, and settings without uninstalling.
3 Option C — Delete individual recordings within the app: Open the app, long-press any button and select Delete. You can remove recordings one by one or through the management screen.
Note: Deleting data via Options A or B is permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure to back up any recordings you want to keep before proceeding.

Advertising Data

The app displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use the Android Advertising ID to show relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads on your device
  • Tap Reset advertising ID or enable Opt out of Ads Personalization

For more information, see Google's Advertising Privacy Policy .
