Privacy Policy for Word Learner

Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Word Learner application handles permissions and processes data during use of the app.

2. Data Collection

Word Learner is designed with user privacy as a priority. The application:

  • Does not collect any personal data
  • Does not require user registration or account creation
  • Does not track your identity or behavior
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services

3. Internet Access and Word Content

The application may connect to an online server (Supabase) solely to fetch word lists and vocabulary content. This connection:

  • Does not send any personal data to the server
  • Is used only to download word content for the learning levels
  • Does not identify or track individual users

4. Permissions Used

The application requires the following device permission:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send optional learning reminders. Notifications can be disabled at any time through your device settings.

Important: This permission is used solely to deliver reminders. No permission is used to collect, store, or transmit personal data.

5. Local Data Storage

Learning progress and app settings are stored locally on your device using DataStore. No personal data is transmitted to external servers.

  • Learning progress is stored only on your device
  • No data is backed up to any server
  • Uninstalling the app removes all stored data

6. What the Application Does NOT Do

To protect user privacy, the application explicitly states that it:

  • Does not collect personal data of any kind
  • Does not require or support user login or registration
  • Does not access your contacts, messages, photos, or other personal files
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not record audio or access your camera
  • Does not share any data with third parties
  • Does not include hidden background tracking or recording behavior

7. User Control and Rights

Users have full control over their data and permissions:

  • Notification permission can be revoked at any time via device settings
  • Learning progress can be cleared by clearing the app's data in device settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data

8. Children's Privacy

Word Learner is suitable for users of all ages. The application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If changes are made to how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
