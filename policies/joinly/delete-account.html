Delete Account & Data - Stretko

This page explains how to delete your account in the Stretko app (developer: Marcel Šoták, package com.joinly.app) and all associated data.

Option 1: Directly in the app (recommended)

1 Open the Stretko app and sign in.
2 Open Settings.
3 Tap Delete account and confirm. The account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request by email (if you no longer have the app)

1 Send an email to marcel.sotak123@gmail.com from the email address you registered with.
2 Use the subject "Stretko account deletion". If you signed in with Google, include your Google account address.
Send account deletion request
Processing time: email requests are handled within 30 days. To protect your account, we may verify that the request comes from its owner.

What Gets Deleted

  • Profile (name, email, bio, profile photo, preferred categories)
  • Login account (Firebase Authentication)
  • Events you created, including their chat
  • Your messages in event chats and your direct messages
  • Your event attendance and waitlist spots
  • Friends, friend requests, invitations and notifications
  • Reports you submitted
  • Images uploaded in the app (Cloudinary) – on request by email within 30 days, not yet deleted automatically

What May Remain

  • Reports other users submitted about your content, and the suspension record if the account was suspended for breaking the rules – for at most 1 year, to review the case
  • Anonymous statistics and crash reports (Google Analytics, Crashlytics) that do not identify you – per Google's retention periods
  • The copy of data on your phone – removed when you uninstall the app

Deleting Only Some Data

If you don't want to delete your whole account, you can at any time edit or remove your profile photo and bio, delete your own events, or leave events in the app. You can request deletion of other specific data (e.g. images) by email.
